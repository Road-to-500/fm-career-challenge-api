# Official objective acceptance matrix

This is the V1 scoring acceptance target, not a claim that every condition is implemented. The source is the original V2 specification, section 10; V3 supersedes conflicting behavior. Points and supported manual frequencies shown are defaults and can be changed for a challenge. Frequency is per participant and challenge and uses the claim's effective rule version, including earlier eligible records. Manual results are participant attestations, not independently verified game data.

All manual claims require an enabled rule and a season. `Per club` and `Club season` additionally require a club name; `Per country` requires a country code. A shared, manually populated club catalog can be linked to career stints and achievement claims. Scoring resolves matching unlinked records to catalog IDs only when the name and country identify a single catalog club; ambiguous names remain text-based. Without a country, a name conservatively matches any country with that name. Frequency, effective rule versions, the exclusions noted below and attestations for G001-G032, G034-G036, G038-G040, G048, G050 and G051 are enforced for new and corrected claims. Historical claims remain scored without attestations and are identified in the score breakdown. A missing validation must not be interpreted as a completed acceptance criterion.

## Verification

`tests/FmCareerChallenge.UnitTests/RaceScoringEngineTests.cs` covers frequency, rule versions, existing exclusions, derived club/country/title milestones, national-team milestone attestation, first bonuses, duels, checkpoints and winning chronology. Every G001-G053 has a base-points test and a disabled-rule test. PostgreSQL integration tests exercise rule selection and challenge isolation. Correction coverage includes domestic competition participation and promotion evidence, continental participation and distinct-club title attestations, international competition kinds and results, and selected career milestones and awards. The domestic double API test also verifies scoring and rejects create/correction payloads contradicted by linked same-season title or cup claims. Persisted correction evidence is checked for continental participation and international results.

The acceptance suite is broad but does not yet test every invalid-input permutation and create/correction pair independently for all 53 objectives. Remaining evidence limitations are described in the objective rows: promotion competition identity is not recorded; the competition catalog is limited to continental competitions; G032's two titles are self-attested rather than cross-validated against competition claims; and match/result attestations are not independently verified against Football Manager data.

Latest local V1 verification: 100 backend unit tests and 55 PostgreSQL integration tests passed against an isolated fresh database; the EF model check found no pending migration changes. All 68 frontend unit tests and the production TypeScript/Vite build passed. All 12 Playwright functional cases passed across desktop/mobile and dark/light configurations with `FMCC_SKIP_ACCESSIBILITY=true`; no axe, keyboard-accessibility, or reduced-motion checks were run in this final verification.

Administrators can maintain a challenge-scoped continental competition catalog with a self-assigned tier. Supported continental claims may optionally link to it; this does not independently verify results or guarantee the tier is correct. Legacy and unlinked claims remain valid and use conservative exclusions. The catalog does not yet cover domestic, national-team, super-cup or world competitions.

Club catalog identities are unique by normalized name, country and optional identity label. If multiple clubs share a name and country, text-only historical records are not automatically linked to any of them; explicit catalog IDs remain stable. For scoring, ambiguous text conservatively matches a possible linked identity until the participant explicitly links the record. When duplicate catalog identities exist, a career stint must select an identity; existing text-only stints must be explicitly linked before another same-name stint can be added or changed. This prevents adding a second career identity from silently changing the eligibility of previously combined stints.

Club matches are grouped by catalog identity where available, or by normalized club name and country across recorded stints. If active detailed season records account for every official match in the relevant stints, the 20-match season is calculated from them. Otherwise a player attests the milestone on a stint active during that season, with enough recorded matches. Editing season allocations or stints can change the milestone and recalculates scoring. Match records themselves are self-reported.

Country milestones use the same complete-record or attestation model, summing all stints in the same country. A qualifying club milestone also qualifies that club's country when country allocations are incomplete. A seeded country/continent catalog is available for selection, but legacy and manually entered country codes remain text-based and are not yet validated against it.

The G048 derived count uses G001-G004, G008-G009, G020-G022, G024, G034 and G035 claims, the same classification as the victory tiebreak. Only claims enabled under their effective rule version are counted; this may omit trophies not recorded or excluded by challenge rules. A manual G048 attestation covers those cases.
